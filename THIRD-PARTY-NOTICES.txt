Altered SDL

RetroEng.Native's win-x64 SDL3.dll is NOT the original SDL. It is SDL at
revision a8591d943b7079b17fdd018dc04ec9c71dc94ae4 with one patch to the
Direct3D 12 backend of SDL_GPU (descriptor-heap rollover before a draw writes
its descriptor tables). The patch is under the same zlib terms as SDL.
